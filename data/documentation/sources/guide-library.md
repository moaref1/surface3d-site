# Material library

The **Assets** panel holds your materials, smart tools, textures, HDRIs and models, plus free CC0 materials from **ambientCG** and **Poly Haven**, ready to download from inside the app.

## Use a material

- Drag a material onto a layer or straight onto the model.
- Or select a fill layer and search the library from its material setting.
- Fill layers show their material on a small sphere, in Layers and in Properties.

## Online materials

1. Open **Assets** and switch to the online source.
2. Search or browse. Materials you already downloaded show a small **downloaded** badge.
3. Drag one onto a layer. It downloads once and stays in your library.

## Your own library

- **Choose a library folder** opens the normal Windows Explorer window, with the address bar, Quick access and search.
- Save any layer or material to Assets from its right-click menu, to reuse it in other projects.
- **File > Import** (**Ctrl+I**) brings in meshes, images, materials and HDRIs.
