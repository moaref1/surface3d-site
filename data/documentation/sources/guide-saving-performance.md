# Saving, performance and limits

## Saving and recovery

- **Ctrl+S** saves the project. Files are compressed without losing anything.
- **Autosave** keeps copies while you work. Find them in **File > Recover > Autosaves...**; change how often it saves in **Preferences > Save & Load**.
- If Surface3D closes unexpectedly, it offers to recover your work at the next start.
- Projects from older versions open normally. A project saved in a newer version may not open in an older one, so keep backups of important work.

## Big projects and 4K

- 4K texturing stays smooth on graphics cards with 8 GB of video memory.
- Sliders, Levels, fill colours and node values respond quickly at 4K.
- Close other GPU-heavy apps while you work at 4K.

## Graphics card

Surface3D uses **Vulkan** by default and falls back automatically if that fails. You can choose the backend in **Preferences > Graphics card**. Keep your graphics driver up to date.

## Known limits in 0.94

- Transparency blending is not in this version.
- A Layers or Properties panel left floating returns to its dock at the next start.
- Library imports: unticked types come back after a rescan.
- Linux is a beta. Reports from different graphics cards are welcome on Discord.

Ask in the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/K3YzGP72cg). For a bug, include your app version, operating system, graphics card, the steps to reproduce it, and what you expected to happen.
