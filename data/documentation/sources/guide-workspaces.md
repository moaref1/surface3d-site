# Workspaces

The top bar switches workspaces. Each keeps its own panel layout, and the status bar shows the hint for the current one.

| Workspace | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Paint** | Layers, brushes, fills, masks, sculpting, splines, particles, decals, edge damage and weathering |
| **Bake** | Mesh maps: normal, AO, curvature, world-space normal, position, thickness and ID, with a cage |
| **UV** | Inspect, unwrap and pack UV islands |
| **Rendering** | Cameras, lights, HDRI environment, turntable, depth of field and post-processing |
| **Graph** | Procedural materials and masks built from nodes, with the full window for the graph |

## Panels

- Panels dock anywhere. Drag a tab to move it; secondary windows open as tabs next to the panel they belong to.
- **Layers** is the first tab of the right column, with **Properties** below it.
- **Assets** on the left holds materials, smart tools, textures, HDRIs and models. It folds into an icon rail when narrow.

## Preferences

- **Interface**: theme and resolution scale (also **Ctrl+=**, **Ctrl+-**, **Ctrl+0** anywhere).
- **Themes** are `.s3dtheme` files in the `themes` folder next to the exe; copy one to share it.
- **Navigation**: choose how orbit, pan and zoom work in the viewport.
- **Keymap**: rebind every shortcut.
