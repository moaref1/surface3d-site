# Welcome to Surface3D

Choose a topic above or start with a project. You can use layers to paint directly, add procedural graphs when you need them, then export textures for your 3D workflow.

## Your first material

1. Import your model and select a texture set.
2. Check the UVs before painting.
3. Add a fill layer for the base material.
4. Add masks and painted details.
5. Bake mesh maps when your material needs surface information such as curvature or occlusion.
6. Review the result and export your textures.
