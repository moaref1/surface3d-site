Questions

Who needs a Studio License?

A company, studio or team where two or more people use Surface3D, or where it runs on computers the company provides. Working alone, even for paying clients, stays free.

Can a studio try it first?

Yes. Studios can evaluate Surface3D for 30 days before buying.

Schools and non-profits?

Ask for a free or reduced Studio License.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once per seat and keep using your version forever. The first year of updates is included; after that, updates are an optional $59 per seat per year.

What does a seat mean?

One named person or one workstation.

How long is the early access price?

For early buyers while Surface3D is in active early development. The price you pay stays yours.

Where are the full terms?

In LICENSE.txt, which also ships with every download.
